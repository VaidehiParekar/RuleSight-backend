# Central Banking Authority (CBA)
## Master Direction — Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering Directions, 2024 (as amended up to 15 June 2025)

**Reference:** CBA/KYC/2024/MD-01 — Amendment No. 1 (CBA/KYC/2025-26/AMD-01)
**Version:** 2.0
**Date of issue of amendment:** 15 June 2025
**Effective date of amendment:** 1 October 2025
**Applicable to:** All Scheduled Commercial Banks regulated by the CBA

---

### Section 1 — Short title and commencement

1.1 These Directions shall be called the CBA (Know Your Customer and Anti-Money Laundering) Directions, 2024.

1.2 These Directions shall come into force on the date of issue. The amendments made by Amendment No. 1 shall come into force on 1 October 2025.

### Section 2 — Applicability

2.1 These Directions apply to every Scheduled Commercial Bank (hereinafter "the bank") operating in India.

2.2 Where a bank has branches or subsidiaries outside India, the bank shall apply the stricter of the requirements of these Directions and the host-country requirements to such branches and subsidiaries.

### Section 3 — Customer Acceptance Policy

3.1 Every bank shall have a Customer Acceptance Policy (CAP) approved by its Board of Directors. The CAP shall be reviewed by the Board at least once a year.

3.2 No account shall be opened in an anonymous or fictitious name, or on behalf of a person whose identity has not been verified.

### Section 4 — Risk categorisation

4.1 The bank shall categorise every customer as Low, Medium or High risk, based on parameters including customer identity, social and financial status, nature of business activity, and geographic location.

4.2 The risk categorisation of every customer shall be reviewed at least once every six (6) months.

### Section 5 — Customer Due Diligence (CDD)

5.1 The bank shall verify the identity and address of every individual customer using an Officially Valid Document (OVD) before opening an account.

5.2 Verification may be carried out through electronic KYC (e-KYC) authentication, through Video-based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP) in accordance with Section 5A, or through physical verification of the OVD.

5.3 Where the customer is a company, partnership firm or trust, the bank shall identify and verify the beneficial owner(s), namely every natural person who holds an ownership interest of **more than ten per cent (10%)** in the customer. *[Amended by Amendment No. 1; earlier "twenty-five per cent (25%)".]*

5.4 *[Deleted by Amendment No. 1.]*

### Section 5A — Video-based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP) *[Inserted by Amendment No. 1]*

5A.1 A bank that onboards customers through V-CIP shall ensure that every V-CIP session:
  (a) includes a **liveness check** to confirm that the customer is physically present and is not a pre-recorded or synthetic video;
  (b) captures the **geo-location** of the customer and confirms that the customer is physically present in India; and
  (c) is conducted by an official of the bank specifically trained for V-CIP.

5A.2 The complete audio-visual recording of every V-CIP session shall be stored **in a system located in India** and shall be preserved for the period specified in Section 9.

### Section 6 — Periodic updation of KYC

6.1 The bank shall carry out periodic updation of KYC records at least once every **two (2) years for High risk** customers, once every **eight (8) years for Medium risk** customers, and once every **ten (10) years for Low risk** customers, from the date of account opening or the date of the last KYC updation.

### Section 7 — Ongoing transaction monitoring

7.1 The bank shall monitor customer transactions on an ongoing basis to identify transactions that are unusual or inconsistent with the customer's profile.

7.2 Every alert generated by the transaction monitoring system shall be examined and disposed of, with recorded reasons, within **thirty (30) days** of generation.

### Section 8 — Reporting to the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU)

8.1 The bank shall furnish a Cash Transaction Report (CTR) of all cash transactions of value more than ₹10,00,000 (Rupees ten lakh), or its equivalent in foreign currency, for each month by the **7th day of the succeeding month**. *[Amended by Amendment No. 1; earlier "15th day".]*

8.2 The bank shall furnish a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) within **seven (7) working days** of arriving at a conclusion that a transaction, or a series of transactions, is suspicious.

### Section 9 — Record keeping

9.1 The bank shall preserve records of all transactions, and records of the identity and address of its customers, for a period of **at least five (5) years** after the business relationship has ended or the account has been closed, whichever is later.

### Section 10 — Principal Officer and Designated Director

10.1 The bank shall appoint a Principal Officer, responsible for furnishing information to the FIU, and a Designated Director, responsible for overall compliance with these Directions. The names and contact details of both shall be communicated to the FIU within fifteen (15) days of appointment.

### Section 11 — Training

11.1 The bank shall conduct KYC/AML training for all front-line, operations and compliance staff **at least once every year**, and shall keep records of attendance.
