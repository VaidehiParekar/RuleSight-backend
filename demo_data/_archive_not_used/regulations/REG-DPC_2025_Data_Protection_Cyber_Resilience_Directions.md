# Central Banking Authority (CBA)
## Directions on Customer Data Protection and Cyber Resilience, 2025

**Reference:** CBA/DPC/2025/MD-02
**Version:** 1.0
**Date of issue:** 1 January 2025
**Effective date:** 1 April 2025
**Applicable to:** All Scheduled Commercial Banks regulated by the CBA

---

### Section 1 — Short title and commencement

1.1 These Directions shall be called the CBA (Customer Data Protection and Cyber Resilience) Directions, 2025.

### Section 2 — Applicability

2.1 These Directions apply to every Scheduled Commercial Bank operating in India, in respect of all customer personal data that the bank collects, processes or stores, whether directly or through a third-party service provider.

### Section 3 — Data minimisation and retention

3.1 The bank shall collect only the customer personal data that is necessary for the stated purpose.

3.2 Customer personal data, including identity documents and records of identity verification, **shall not be retained for more than three (3) years after the closure of the customer's account**, and shall be securely erased at the end of that period.

### Section 4 — Encryption

4.1 The bank shall encrypt all customer personal data **at rest**, using AES-256 or an equivalent or stronger algorithm, and **in transit**, using TLS 1.2 or a later version.

### Section 5 — Privileged access management

5.1 The bank shall review all privileged user access to systems that store or process customer personal data **at least once every quarter**, and shall revoke access that is no longer required.

### Section 6 — Cyber incident reporting

6.1 The bank shall report every cyber security incident that affects customer data or critical banking services to the CBA **within six (6) hours** of detecting the incident.

### Section 7 — Authentication

7.1 The bank shall enforce **multi-factor authentication (MFA)** for (a) all remote access to the bank's network and (b) all privileged access to production systems, whether remote or on-premises.

### Section 8 — Governance

8.1 The bank shall have an Information Security Policy approved by its Board of Directors, and the Board shall review the policy at least once a year.

### Section 9 — Security logs

9.1 The bank shall retain security and audit logs of all systems that process customer personal data for **at least one hundred and eighty (180) days**, in a tamper-evident manner.

### Section 10 — Third-party service providers

10.1 Where customer personal data (including audio-visual recordings of customers) is stored or processed by a third-party service provider, the bank shall ensure, through its contract, that:
  (a) the data is **stored only in India**; and
  (b) the service provider is **audited at least once a year** by the bank or by an independent auditor appointed by the bank.

*End of Directions.*
