# Nova Trust Bank Ltd.
## Standard Operating Procedure — Currency Management and Note Sorting Machines

**Document ID:** POL-04
**Version:** 2.1
**Owner:** Head – Currency Management
**Approved by:** Operations Committee, 10 June 2025

---

### 1. Scope

1.1 This SOP applies to note sorting machines (NSMs) and note authentication machines **installed in the Bank's branches and currency chests**.

1.2 Cash processing done by the Bank's cash-in-transit (CIT) agency, SecureCash Logistics Pvt. Ltd., at its own cash-processing hubs is covered by the CIT service agreement and is **outside the scope of this SOP**.

### 2. Controls

**CTL-19 — Machine testing and calibration**
Each NSM is tested for accuracy **once every six months** (April and October) using a test deck of **at least 1,000 notes** made up of soiled, mutilated and counterfeit specimens. If the machine misclassifies more than 5% of notes, the vendor recalibrates it. The branch manager signs a test certificate that is kept at the branch.
*Owner:* Branch Manager / Currency Management. *Frequency:* Half-yearly.

**CTL-20 — Procurement of note sorting machines**
Since 1 May 2025, the Bank buys only NSM models certified by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) under IS 18663:2024. Procurement checks the BIS licence number before raising a purchase order.
*Owner:* Procurement. *Frequency:* Per purchase.

**CTL-21 — Authentication and fitness configuration**
The machine vendor configures every machine to RBI's authentication features and fitness-sorting parameters, including sorting phased-out note series as unfit. When RBI changes a parameter, Currency Management asks the vendor to update all machines within 30 days.
*Owner:* Currency Management / Vendor. *Frequency:* On RBI notification.

**CTL-22 — Suspect and reject notes**
Notes classified by a machine as suspect or reject are kept aside and inspected manually by the cash officer at the end of each day.
*Owner:* Cash Officer. *Frequency:* Daily.

### 3. Machine inventory

3.1 Currency Management keeps an inventory of all machines with model, BIS certification status, and last test date. *(The SOP has no plan to replace older machine models that are no longer manufactured.)*
