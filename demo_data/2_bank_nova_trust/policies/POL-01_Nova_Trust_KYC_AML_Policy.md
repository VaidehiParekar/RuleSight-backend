# Nova Trust Bank Ltd.
## KYC and Anti-Money Laundering Policy

**Document ID:** POL-01
**Version:** 3.2
**Owner:** Chief Compliance Officer
**Approved by:** Board of Directors, 12 March 2024
**Governing rules:** RBI Master Direction – Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016 (updated 14 August 2025)

---

### 1. Scope

1.1 This Policy sets out how Nova Trust Bank Ltd. ("the Bank") applies the RBI KYC Master Direction to its customers.

1.2 The Bank operates 142 branches, all located in India. The Bank has no branches or subsidiaries outside India.

### 2. Controls

**CTL-02 — Customer risk categorisation review**
At account opening, the core banking rule engine assigns every customer a risk category of Low, Medium or High. The Compliance – KYC Unit reviews the risk category of all customers **once a year**, in April.
*Owner:* Compliance – KYC Unit. *Frequency:* Annual review.

**CTL-04 — Beneficial ownership identification**
For companies, the Bank identifies and verifies every natural person who holds **more than 25%** ownership interest in the customer, using the shareholding pattern.
*Owner:* Corporate Banking Operations. *Frequency:* Per account opening.

**CTL-05 — Periodic KYC updation**
The Bank re-performs KYC for existing customers once every **three (3) years for High risk** customers, once every eight (8) years for Medium risk customers, and once every ten (10) years for Low risk customers. The core banking system generates a due list every month.
*Owner:* Compliance – KYC Unit. *Frequency:* Monthly due list.

**CTL-09 — Record retention**
The Bank retains transaction records and customer identity records for **eight (8) years** after the account is closed, after which they are archived offline.
*Owner:* Records Management. *Frequency:* Continuous.

**CTL-10 — Principal Officer and Designated Director**
The Bank has appointed a Principal Officer and a Designated Director, and their details are communicated to the FIU-IND.
*Owner:* Company Secretary. *Frequency:* Event-driven.
*(This control does not state whether the two appointments are held by different persons.)*
