# Nova Trust Bank Ltd.
## Standard Operating Procedure — Digital Account Onboarding (Video KYC)

**Document ID:** POL-03
**Version:** 1.4
**Owner:** Head – Digital Banking
**Approved by:** Digital Banking Committee, 5 August 2024

---

### 1. Purpose

1.1 This SOP describes how the Bank opens savings accounts for individual customers through its mobile app, using video-based customer identification ("Video KYC").

### 2. Process

2.1 The customer completes the application in the mobile app and schedules a video call.

2.2 The video call is conducted through **"ClearView Video KYC"**, a platform operated by a third-party vendor, ClearView Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

**CTL-17 — Video KYC session**
During the call, a Bank agent:
  (a) asks the customer to show the original OVD on camera;
  (b) compares the customer's face with the photograph on the OVD; and
  (c) asks the customer three security questions.
Bank agents complete the vendor's standard product training before handling calls.
*Owner:* Digital Banking Operations. *Frequency:* Per session.

**CTL-18 — Video KYC recording storage**
Every video call is recorded. Recordings are stored on ClearView's cloud storage and are retained for eight (8) years. *(The SOP does not state where the vendor's cloud storage is located.)* The vendor contract was signed in July 2024.
*Owner:* Digital Banking Operations / Vendor Management. *Frequency:* Per session.
